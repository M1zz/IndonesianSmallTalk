About Malmun

Malmun helps you practise real conversations through branching dialogues. Choose a language to learn — Indonesian, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish or Korean — and use the app itself in any of those same six languages.

💬
Branching small talk
The conversation goes where your answers take it. Practise by reading and tapping, or by speaking out loud.
🧳
53 situational dialogues
In every language: the basics, casual chat, restaurants, getting around, the airport, hotels, shopping, sightseeing and emergencies.
🇮🇩
Extra for Indonesian
Hand-written small talk, three day courses with a weekly timetable, and a 100-word starter vocabulary.
🚗
Commute Mode
Practise by ear in the car over Bluetooth or CarPlay. The audio plays through your car speakers and the sentence appears in Now Playing. You hear the meaning, then a chime, then it's your turn to say it, and finally the answer, slowly and at normal speed. There's no separate CarPlay app screen.
🗣️
Hands-free control
Use the next, previous and play buttons on your steering wheel, or speak during your turn: next, again, slower, faster, next scene, stop. Say the line correctly and you'll hear "Nice!". Start it with Siri, or automatically with a Shortcuts automation when you connect to the car.
🎯
Practice tools
Pronunciation practice with a score, script view, importing dialogues by pasting them in, and practice history.
📚
My phrases & flashcards
Save the phrases you pick up and revise vocabulary with flashcards that read the words aloud.
⌨️
Phrase keyboard
See the meaning, tap once, and the sentence is typed out in the language you're learning.
👥
Share with friends
Share scenarios with friends through iCloud.
✨
AI scenarios
Add your own Anthropic API key, type in a topic and get a brand-new dialogue.

Privacy

No account and no ad tracking. What you study stays on your device, and in iCloud if you turn sharing on. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics and error reports so that it can be fixed and improved.
